Frequently Asked Questions

How do I inquire about purchasing an artwork?

Contact us via email or the contact form above, mentioning which artwork interests you. We'll provide pricing, availability, and shipping information.

Do you offer custom commissions?

Yes! We work with our featured artists on custom projects. Contact us to discuss your vision and requirements.

Are there group visit options?

Absolutely. Groups of 10 or more can schedule guided tours. Educational programs available for schools and institutions.

What is the gallery's stance on psychological themes?

We explore psychological concepts responsibly, with artistic integrity. Our work promotes understanding, not harmful behavior.

Can I take photographs in the gallery?

Personal photography is permitted for most exhibitions. Flash photography is prohibited. Contact us for exceptions.

How do I become a member?

Members receive free admission, exclusive previews, and member-only events. Contact us at hello@beautyindisorder.art for membership details.
