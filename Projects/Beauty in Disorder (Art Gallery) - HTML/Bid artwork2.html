About This Piece

Void Within explores the psychological phenomenon of emotional numbness and affective detachment. This artwork represents the internal emptiness experienced by those who have learned to suppress or dissociate from their emotions.

The piece challenges viewers to confront the aesthetic paradox: can emptiness itself be beautiful? Can absence of feeling be represented as presence in art?
