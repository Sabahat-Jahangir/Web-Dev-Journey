Current & Upcoming Exhibitions

Immerse yourself in our curated explorations of the human psyche

Currently on Display

Fragmentation: The Splintered Mind

Dates: January 15 - March 30, 2024

Location: Main Gallery, Beauty in Disorder

This powerful exhibition explores the concept of cognitive fragmentation and mental dissociation. Featuring 12 major works by Marcus Void and guest artists, Fragmentation examines how the unified sense of self can splinter under psychological pressure.

Featured Themes:
Cognitive Dissonance
Identity Dissolution
Divided Consciousness

The Void: Beauty in Absence

Dates: February 1 - April 15, 2024

Location: Lower Gallery, Beauty in Disorder

The Void is a haunting exploration of emotional detachment and the paradox of finding beauty in emptiness. This exhibition challenges viewers to confront their own relationships with absence, numbness, and psychological void.

Featured Themes:
Emotional Numbness
The Beauty of Absence
Affective Detachment
