About This Piece

Fragmented Mind is a visual representation of how consciousness splinters when overwhelmed by competing thoughts and emotions. The piece showcases the phenomenon of dissociation and mental fragmentation—how the unified sense of self can dissolve into multiple, sometimes contradictory fragments.

This artwork explores the psychological concept of cognitive dissonance and the internal conflict that arises when different aspects of the psyche pull in opposite directions. The layered composition represents the different "selves" that exist within one consciousness.

Psychological Themes

Cognitive Dissonance - The tension between conflicting thoughts
Dissociation - The splitting of consciousness into separate parts
Internal Conflict - The struggle between different psychological aspects
Identity Fragmentation - The dissolution of a unified self
